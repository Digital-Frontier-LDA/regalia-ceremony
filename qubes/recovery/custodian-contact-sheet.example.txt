  SEALED CUSTODIAN-CONTACT SHEET   (one sealed in EVERY case)
  It names the EXECUTOR, never the other holders. Keep sealed.

This sheet tells whoever holds this case whom to contact: the executor.
It deliberately does NOT list the other cases or their holders: one
stolen or coerced case must not lead to the rest. The executor alone
holds the directory of cases (kept by a notary or lawyer, and
encrypted), and receives it only when the trigger below has happened.

    [e.g. "death or medical incapacity of the owner, confirmed by the
     executor; or written instruction from the owner"]            <-- fill in

    Backup:   [second executor]

    Name:     [____]
    Reach:    [____]
    Fallback: [reputable crypto-recovery firm]

SAFE DESTINATION for swept funds:
    [akash1... cold address, or "executor will provide at recovery time"]

THIS CASE ONLY:
    Case ID:       [DF-BG-0x]
    Holo serial:   [HOLO-00000x]

NOTES

    then rotate.
  - Hand this case over ONLY as the share form in it says: on the

  - Keep this sheet sealed inside the case; do not photograph it.
